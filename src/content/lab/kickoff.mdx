## Overview

Kickoff is a football manager for the browser — but deliberately on the opposite
end of the effort spectrum from something like Football Manager. Where those
games ask for hours, Kickoff is built to be minimal and low-effort: everything
you need to run your club is one glance away, no dropdowns, no buried menus.

My friend [Alexander Karlsson](https://farcaster.xyz/acgk.eth) vibecoded the
first version. I joined to redesign it — and ended up doing what I should have
from the start: ignoring the original design entirely and rebuilding the whole
experience from the ground up. The always-persistent navbar became the anchor of
the new visual language, every nav item a state selector rather than a button
(a rabbit hole I wrote about in [naming things
properly](/essays/on-the-importance-of-naming-things-properly)).

It's a two-person passion project, and the part that pushes past pure design is
the handoff: designs move to working code with a backend-dev collaborator, LLMs
doing the heavy lifting on the design-to-code translation while we shape the game
logic underneath. Me designing the thing, then getting it built — for no one but
ourselves.
