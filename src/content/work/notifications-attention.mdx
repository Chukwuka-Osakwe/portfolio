## The ask

"Engagement is down — can we send more push notifications?" Volume was the
proposed lever. I suspected volume was the problem, not the cure.

## What I found

Opt-out rates spiked the day after high-volume sends. We were spending
long-term permission to buy short-term opens.

## The decision

I designed a relevance budget: each user has a finite "interrupt allowance," and
a notification has to clear a bar to spend it. Low-value pings got batched into a
daily digest instead.

## Outcome

Total sends dropped; opens *per send* and retention both rose. **The right to
interrupt is earned, then spent carefully.**
