## The ask

"Add the new features to the pricing table." The table already had forty rows
across three plans. Adding more would have buried the decision entirely.

## The decision

I treated this as an **editing** problem, not a layout one. I worked with sales
and product to find the few attributes that actually drove the choice, and
demoted the rest behind a "full comparison" expander.

## How it went

- Default view: the handful of dimensions that change a buyer's mind.
- Full table: still available, one click away, for the detail-seekers.
- Plain-language plan summaries above the grid.

## Outcome

Self-serve conversions rose and pricing questions to sales dropped. **Clarity
is mostly subtraction.**
