## The ask

"Search feels slow — can we speed up the backend?" Engineering had already
shaved latency; the numbers were good. The complaint persisted.

## What I found

The wait wasn't long, it was *blank*. A spinner on an empty screen makes any
delay feel like a stall. The problem was perception, not milliseconds.

## The decision

I designed for perceived speed instead of raw speed:

1. Instant feedback on keystroke (the input never feels dead).
2. Skeleton results that match the real layout, so the page feels populated.
3. Recent and suggested queries fill the empty state.

## Outcome

Measured latency was unchanged; *perceived* speed rose sharply, and the
complaints stopped. **Sometimes the fix isn't faster — it's less blank.**
