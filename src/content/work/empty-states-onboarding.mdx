## The ask

"Our onboarding tour has low completion — can we make it shippier?" The tour was
a sequence of coach-marks people clicked through without reading.

## What I found

The moment that actually mattered came *after* the tour: the first empty screen,
where people had to do something real and had no idea what.

## The decision

I treated every primary empty state as a teaching surface — a clear first action,
a sample to react to, and a one-line "why this matters" — and trimmed the tour to
almost nothing.

## Outcome

Activation rose without the tour doing the heavy lifting. **People learn a
product by using it, so design the moment they first do.**
