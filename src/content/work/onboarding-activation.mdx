## The ask

Activation was flat, and the assumption was that people didn't understand the
product. The proposed fix: a richer, longer tutorial.

## What I found

The opposite was true. A nine-step setup wizard front-loaded *configuration*
before anyone had seen a single result. Most drop-off happened on step three —
people quit before the product had earned the right to ask for more.

## The decision

I cut the wizard to **one** required step and deferred everything else until
after the first meaningful result. Configuration became progressive, surfaced
in context when it actually mattered.

## Outcome

Time-to-first-value dropped from minutes to seconds, and activation followed.
The principle: **earn input by showing value first.**
