## The ask

"Add these six metrics to the dashboard." The dashboard already had eleven. It
had become a place to *store* data, not read it.

## What I found

In interviews, almost everyone opened the dashboard to check **one** thing —
but which one differed by role. The wall of widgets served no one well.

## The decision

I led with a single, role-aware headline metric and a one-line "why it changed,"
then demoted everything else into a scannable second tier.

## Outcome

Time-to-answer dropped sharply, and the "add more widgets" requests slowed —
people trusted the dashboard to surface what mattered. **A dashboard is an
answer, not a warehouse.**
